About Judi Glam House

Judi Glam House is a beauty studio specializing in African braids, locks, and nail artistry. Founded with passion, culture, and creativity, our mission is to make every client feel confident, elegant, and empowered.

Our studio blends African heritage with modern beauty techniques. Whether you want clean men braids, long women tresses, natural locks, or detailed nail designs, we deliver precision, comfort, and long‑lasting results.

At Judi Glam House, beauty is more than a service — it’s an experience.
